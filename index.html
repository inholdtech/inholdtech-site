AI readiness & security

Adopt AI without exposing your data.

AI assistants can surface anything a user has access to, and staff already paste company data into public AI tools. We prepare your data, control how AI is used and give you visibility, whichever AI tools you choose.

  • AI data readiness: fix oversharing, permissions and sensitivity labels before AI assistants such as Microsoft Copilot or Gemini can surface sensitive files
  • AI data security posture: see which AI tools are used and what sensitive data goes into them
  • Shadow AI control: discover and govern unapproved AI apps and stop sensitive data being pasted into public AI tools
  • Secure AI rollout: deploy Copilot, Gemini, ChatGPT Enterprise or Claude with SSO, access control and audit logging
  • AI governance: acceptable-use policy, audit and retention for AI interactions

Data protection & compliance

Meet NCA and PDPL requirements in your cloud.

Regulators across the region expect organisations to protect personal data and prove it. We implement and document the technical controls in Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, and work alongside your legal and compliance team.

  • Saudi NCA ECC: identity and access, logging, email protection, backup and cloud security controls
  • Saudi PDPL: classification, DLP, retention and access to personal data
  • UAE PDPL & Egypt PDPL (Law 151): equivalent technical safeguards
  • Audit-ready evidence: configuration reports and documented changes

FAQ

Common questions

What access do you need?

Reviews start with read-only access granted by your administrator. We don't change anything without your written approval, and access is removed when the work ends.

Doesn't Microsoft or Google already back up my data?

They keep the service running. Recovering from deletion, ransomware or a departed employee is your responsibility, and built-in retention has limits. An independent backup gives you a copy outside the tenant.

Can you help us meet NCA ECC or PDPL requirements?

Yes. We implement and document the technical controls in Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, such as access control, logging, data classification, DLP, retention and backup, and provide evidence for audits. Legal interpretation stays with your legal or compliance advisers, and we work alongside them.

How do we get started?

Book a consultation. We discuss your environment and goals, agree the scope, then begin with a read-only review so recommendations are based on your actual configuration.
