FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer on-site support?

Not yet. Everything we do is delivered remotely, which covers most cloud, security and user-support work. On-site services such as cabling, physical servers and hardware installs are planned for later.

What access do you need for the free health check?

Read-only access, granted by your administrator and removed when the review ends. We don't change anything without your written approval.

Doesn't Microsoft or Google already back up my data?

They keep the service running. Recovering from deletion, ransomware or a departed employee is your responsibility, and built-in retention has limits. An independent backup gives you a copy outside the tenant.

How is pricing structured?

Ongoing support and backup are priced per user per month. Projects such as migrations and hardening are fixed-price after the health check, so you know the cost before you commit.
